Our story

A vineyard where the theatre steps outside.

Vigna Misteriosa is a new urban vineyard at Teatro Franco Parenti: 330 vines, and around them a space for culture, nature and conversation.

Milan moves fast. More and more people come here to study and work, and many are still looking for a place to belong. We want to give them one — somewhere to slow down, meet people and take part, not just watch. For locals, students, newcomers, theatre-goers and wine lovers alike.

“Let the vines set the scene.”

        Vine dedication

        Who would you grow a vine for?

        A birthday gift, a thank-you, a way to remember someone — or a promise to come back together. Available with Rooftop Guardian (€300), Terrace Guardian (€500) and Patron (€1,000).

        A dedication is a symbolic gift. It doesn’t mean ownership of the vine or free access to every area of the vineyard — some zones are not open to the public.
